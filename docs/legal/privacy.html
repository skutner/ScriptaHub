2. Information processed

The AI Librarian compares a question with the catalogue on the visitor’s device. The question is stored in the URL fragment, which browsers do not send to the web server as part of the page request.

Site deliveryThe hosting provider may process IP address, request time, requested path, user agent and security logs to deliver and protect the site. Complete the provider, location and retention period: [HOSTING PROVIDER / COUNTRIES / RETENTION].
Local preferencesLanguage, light or dark theme and text size are stored in localStorage after the visitor chooses them. They stay on that device until removed in browser settings.
AI LibrarianQuestions and ranking calculations remain on the device. No question is submitted to ScriptaHub by the recommendation feature itself.
Optional dictationIf a visitor activates dictation, the browser’s speech-recognition service processes microphone input under the browser provider’s terms. ScriptaHub does not receive the audio.
Reader accountsDownloading through site buttons or leaving edition feedback requests an account. Registration and sign-in take place in the UserPersisto account window. If Google sign-in is enabled and you select it, Google authenticates your Google account and UserPersisto resolves or creates your local account. ScriptaHub receives the account identifier, email and membership roles, and checks membership when an account action is requested. A short-lived UserPersisto access credential is kept in this tab’s sessionStorage only when the requested action continues. ScriptaHub does not receive your password or Google access tokens. Cancelling here prevents this pending ScriptaHub action and credential from being saved; it cannot undo account or sign-in changes already completed on UserPersisto or Google. Reading and direct PDF links remain public. Account-service operator, hosting and account retention: [USERPERSISTO OPERATOR / HOSTING / RETENTION].
Email proposals and feedbackForms open the visitor’s email application. If the visitor sends the message, names, contact details, attachments and message content are processed by the sender’s and recipient’s email providers and by ScriptaHub to answer and evaluate the submission. Proposed retention: [PERIOD].
Reader analyticsThe online reader loads Umami Cloud. Umami documents that its standard tracker uses no cookies and records anonymised traffic metrics such as page views, referrer, browser, operating system, device type and country. Confirm account region, retention, contract and any custom-event configuration: [DETAILS].

3. Purposes and legal bases

Proposed purposes are delivering and securing the site, remembering requested interface preferences, providing requested reader-account actions, answering messages, assessing contributions and understanding aggregate reader use. For visitors in the EEA, record the applicable GDPR legal basis for each purpose, normally performance of a requested service, legitimate interests, consent where required, or steps requested before a contract. For Swiss visitors, processing must follow the principles and information duties of the Federal Act on Data Protection. Complete the operator’s balancing tests and records before relying on legitimate interests.

5. Retention and security

Keep personal data only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal duties and the handling of disputes. Insert concrete periods for server logs, email correspondence, accepted contributions and backups. Use access control, updates, backups and incident procedures appropriate to the risk.
