Predator in the Name of the Dead

A novel in two parts

The novel opens as EIDOLON, an artificial observer sent from Earth, confesses to contaminating Nymba. Its transfer probe accidentally carries Summer-Six, a female varro parasite, between continents whose ecologies had long developed separately. A discrepancy of 7.3 milligrams becomes a civilizational event. The machine initially records a technical deviation rather than a moral action, and help from Earth is decades away.
