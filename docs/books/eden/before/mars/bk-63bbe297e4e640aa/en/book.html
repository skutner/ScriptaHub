Eden Before Mars

If We Can Build Worlds Beyond Earth, We Can Build Paradise Here

The book reads paradise traditions as evidence of what human societies have found intolerable: thirst, exhausting labor, predation, insecurity, hierarchy, illness, and death. Eden, Dilmun, Persian and Qur’anic gardens, blessed landscapes, Arcadia, and the Peach Blossom Spring retain distinct histories and meanings; recurring water, fertility, enclosure, safety, and boundary motifs do not prove a single origin.
