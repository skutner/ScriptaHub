What Matters When AI Can Create Almost Anything

A century ago, a person with an idea faced the problem of making it real. Writing a book required months or years. Testing a scientific hypothesis required scarce instruments and labour. Producing a film consumed capital before an audience could see the first finished version. Even generating a serious design alternative cost time. Those constraints were often unfair, but they gave intellectual production a built-in throttle.
