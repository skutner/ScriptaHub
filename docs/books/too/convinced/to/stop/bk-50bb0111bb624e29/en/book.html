Too Convinced to Stop

Conviction, Reality Distortion and the Right to Be Wrong

The book challenges the habit of calling improbable winners visionaries and failed believers delusional after events have made uncertainty disappear. Outcomes contain skill and judgment, but also timing, capital, geography, disease, market structure, political support, and luck. A false model may meet a fortunate world, while a sound process can lose. The useful question is therefore not whether ambitious people are rational.
