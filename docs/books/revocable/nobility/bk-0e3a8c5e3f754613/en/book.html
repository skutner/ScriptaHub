Revocable Nobility

A Human-AI Inquiry into Scarcity, Legitimacy, and the Social Contract of Elites

The book begins from an intentionally uncomfortable premise: complex societies inevitably concentrate knowledge, coordination, resources, and decision-making power. Denying elites does not abolish them; it hides them behind labels such as merit, leadership, expertise, ownership, security, or innovation. The inquiry is therefore not an argument for revenge against the powerful.
