Life Without an Audience

A Field Manual for Inner Freedom in the Age of Metrics, Machines, and Other Minds

Life Without an Audience is framed as an apprenticeship whose teacher must eventually surrender authority. The imagined teacher does not offer escape from society or a method for becoming indifferent to other people. Human identity is relational, consequences are shared, and no life can be purged of inherited voices, observers, obligations, or the desire to be understood. The problem is jurisdiction.
