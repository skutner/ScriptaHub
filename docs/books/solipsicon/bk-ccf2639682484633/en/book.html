SOLIPSICON

A Philosophical Science-Fiction Novel

SOLIPSICON begins with a brutally efficient premise: beings called children inhabit a higher-dimensional realm, surviving inside fragile Sphero-Nests on Soma-Nectar dispensed by the Loom. They earn nourishment by entering human minds, tilting intentions, and producing consequential events in three-dimensional history. The system rewards resonance, not justice, and never adequately explains what resonance measures.
