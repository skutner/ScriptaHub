Ecology of Predation

From the Physics of Capture to the Society of Artificial Intelligences

The book frames predation as a deliberately limited prototype for relationships in which one system prolongs itself or expands its freedom by capturing resources associated with another. It does not label fixed groups as predators or claim that physical, biological, social, and artificial mechanisms are identical.
