SOLIPSCION

A Science-Fiction Novel

When the adults disappear in the Nulling, KERA and five other children—ORU, SILE, NAH, VAEL, and TAV—survive in separate nacres linked by a damaged Suture. Their bodies and rooms belong to a higher-dimensional order whose geometry no longer behaves reliably. A Tutor offers no comforting explanation. It instructs them to participate in simulated worlds, serve desire, learn consequence, mature, and eventually hatch.
