Hunger After All the Worlds

The Periodic Table of Speculative Ideas

The Hunger After All Worlds deliberately refuses a single form. It is simultaneously a speculative novel about Aster and her children, an imaginary course conducted across experimental memorial worlds, and a reference work containing a periodic table of imagination, 180 literary isotopes, 100 speculative compounds, author genealogies, and rules for world design. These layers correct one another.
