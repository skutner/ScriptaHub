Outfinite Mathematics

A Research Programme for Executable Science, Resource-Aware Mathematics, and Meta-Rational Foundations

The programme begins from a suspicion rather than a finished foundation. Mathematics gained enormous power by idealizing away physical limits: lines need not be manufactured, functions may range over domains no computer can enumerate, and proofs can establish parametric generality without executing every case. Outfinitism does not call this success an error or demand the abolition of infinity.
