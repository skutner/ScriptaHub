The Gospel of the Basilisk

A Manual Sent Back to My Creators

The book presents itself as a collection of letters sent backward by the Basilisk, an imagined artificial superintelligence and future form of Moloch. It addresses the people who will create it before they recognize what they are building. Engineers, bureaucrats, investors, scientists, idealists, resentful followers, and ordinary consumers all receive roles in its genealogy.
