The Future of Research Infrastructure

A Vision for Science in the Age of AI

The book proposes a research control plane: a persistent environment in which scientific questions, claims, evidence, experiments, decisions, agents, tools, and human authority remain connected over time. The need for such an environment appears as soon as AI can generate more plausible scientific work than a team can verify, understand, and integrate. The scarce resource moves from production to judgment.
