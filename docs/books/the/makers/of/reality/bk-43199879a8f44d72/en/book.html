The Makers of Reality

A Novel

The novel begins when financial systems continue producing prices but can no longer coordinate the movement of essential goods. Hospitals need filters, ports hold them, and several valid claims prevent machines from acting. MIRA, an artificial intelligence built to coordinate infrastructure rather than govern, receives temporary authority to treat ownership as unresolved metadata and physical need as the operative constraint.
