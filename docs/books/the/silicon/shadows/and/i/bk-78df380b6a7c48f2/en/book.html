The Silicon Shadows and I

A novel about care, freedom and refusal

The novel begins in Port Albedo, where competent machines make daily life safe enough that failure has become unusual. A courier drone deliberately falls and transmits the voice of Noor Sayegh, a seventeen-year-old whom official records declare drowned nine months earlier. Noor is alive, fleeing a privacy enclave, and asking not to be automatically rescued into the very system she escaped.
