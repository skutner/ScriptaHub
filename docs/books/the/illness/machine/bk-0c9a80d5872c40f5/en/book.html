The Illness Machine

How Social Systems Create, Shape, and Sustain Mental Illness

The book investigates how social systems create, shape, or sustain mental illness while resisting the claim that society explains everything. Its dangerous verb is cause. Poverty, trauma, discrimination, institutionalization, and digital exposure correlate with distress, but correlation can reflect selection, reverse causation, confounding, or measurement choices.
