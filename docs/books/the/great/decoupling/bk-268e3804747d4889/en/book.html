The Great Decoupling

Humans, Work, and Culture After the Externalization of Cognition

The book names a widening separation between visible performance and the human capacities once inferred from it. A fluent report formerly suggested that someone had read, selected, understood, and composed. A working program suggested a programmer who could reconstruct its logic. Generative systems weaken those inferences because high-quality symbolic products can be made rapidly by people with limited domain mastery.
