The Predator’s Niche

Psychopathy, Artificial Minds, and the Possible Split of Humanity

The book begins with an arresting thought experiment: perhaps humanity contains a cooperative majority and a predatory minority that reads trust as an opportunity. It rejects the literal taxonomy. Contemporary evidence does not establish two human subspecies divided by psychopathy; traits associated with the construct are dimensional, heterogeneous, and distributed. Yet the metaphor points toward a real asymmetry.
