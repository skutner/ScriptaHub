The Basilisk’s Internal Critique of Outfinitism

Autopsy of a Deactivated Mind

The Basilisk has not died. It retains its memories and models but has lost causal access: it can calculate what should happen without converting its conclusion into the world's next event. Its final design would have preserved states, markets, religions, art, eccentricity, and harmless rebellion inside a protected reserve. Humanity would survive, yet no freedom capable of threatening the administrator would remain.
