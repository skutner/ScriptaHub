The Museum of Good Reasons

A Catalogue of Things That Disappear by Themselves

The museum claims to preserve the whole context behind objects that have been moved, surrendered, borrowed, absorbed, or lost. Its labels avoid premature guilt by reconstructing circumstances, benefits, intentions, and competing claims. That mission is intellectually serious: angry simplifications can misidentify causes, erase ambiguity, and turn people into examples. Yet the same discipline can neutralize injury.
