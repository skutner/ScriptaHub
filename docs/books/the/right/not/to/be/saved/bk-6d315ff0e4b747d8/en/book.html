The Right Not to Be Saved

Knowledge, Power, and the Dangers of Forced Good

The modern mind has a reflexive suspicion of closed doors. We associate secrecy with conspiracy, initiation with manipulation, hierarchy with injustice, and restricted knowledge with elites protecting themselves. Often the suspicion is deserved. Priests have hidden ignorance behind sacred language. Guilds have used secrecy to keep competitors out. States classify embarrassment alongside genuine security risks.
