The History and Future of Social Technologies

The Yin-Yang of Civilization

Conventional technological history foregrounds durable artifacts: blades, ploughs, engines, ships, computers, and neural networks. This book argues that every such capability enters history within an institutional assembly. Ships require contracts, credit, insurance, navigation standards, and organizations that pool risk.
