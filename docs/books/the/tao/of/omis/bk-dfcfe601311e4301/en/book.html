The Tao of OMIS

The Open Knot · The Gods Who Took Notes · The Soul Between

OMIS names the small interval left inside a functional knot. Pull a knot perfectly tight and it becomes brittle when the load changes; leave a controlled opening and it can adjust without ceasing to bind. The book applies this image to love, institutions, and consciousness. Because closeness inevitably influences people, the ethical goal is not an untouched self but influence that remains answerable.
