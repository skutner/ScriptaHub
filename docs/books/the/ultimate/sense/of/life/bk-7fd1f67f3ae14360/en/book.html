The Ultimate Sense of Life

A Serious Comedy of Gods, Ants, Consciousness, Saints, Sinners, and the Universe Trying to Understand Itself

The book refuses to treat “the meaning of life” as one neatly bounded question. The phrase conceals inquiries about biological function, personal direction, intelligibility, moral value, endurance under suffering, divine intention, and the possible significance of consciousness in the universe. An answer to one can easily impersonate an answer to all.
