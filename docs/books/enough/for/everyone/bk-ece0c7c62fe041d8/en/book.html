Enough for Everyone

The Planet’s Resources, the Limits of Abundance, and the Possible Road to a Post-Scarcity Economy

With reasonable probability, humanity has enough energy, matter, water, and biological capacity for no human being to remain constrained by lack of food, decent housing, basic medical treatment, education, connectivity, or a reasonable degree of mobility. This conclusion assumes neither infinite resources nor miraculous technology.
