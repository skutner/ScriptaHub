Trustworthy AI

From Model Quality to System Assurance

Engineers often encounter justified reliance on AI first as a vocabulary term and only later as a design problem. Accuracy, usefulness, safety and trustworthiness are related but not interchangeable. A system can be accurate on a benchmark and still be unsafe in a workflow because the benchmark says little about users, authority, latency, data quality or consequences.
